💬 Key Phrases — Ключові Фрази
Can I help you?
Чим можу допомогти? (продавець до покупця)
🗣 Seller: "Good morning! Can I help you?" — Buyer: "Yes, I'm looking for a blue jacket."
I'm just looking, thanks.
Я просто дивлюся, дякую.
🗣 Use this when you don't need help yet but want to be polite. / Використовуй, коли ще не готовий купувати.
Do you have this in...?
Чи є у вас це в...?
🗣 "Do you have this in red?" / "Do you have this in a bigger size?"
Where can I find...?
Де я можу знайти...?
🗣 "Excuse me, where can I find the dairy section?" — "It's in aisle 4, sir."
Is this on sale?
Це на розпродажу? / Є знижка на це?
🗣 "Excuse me, is this jacket on sale?" — "Yes, it's 30% off today!"
I'd like to buy...
Я хочу купити...
🗣 "I'd like to buy two bottles of water and some bread, please."
How much is this?
Скільки це коштує?
🗣 "Excuse me, how much is this scarf?" — "It's £15."
That's too expensive.
Це занадто дорого.
🗣 "Hmm, £200 for a bag? That's too expensive for me."
Can I pay by card?
Чи можна сплатити карткою?
🗣 "Can I pay by card or is it cash only?" — "We accept both!"
Do you accept contactless?
Ви приймаєте безконтактну оплату?
🗣 Also: "Do you accept Apple Pay / Google Pay?"
Can I have a receipt?
Чи можу я отримати чек?
🗣 "Can I have a receipt, please?" — "Of course! Here you go."
Keep the change.
Залиш здачу собі.
🗣 When paying cash and you want to leave the change as a tip. / Коли залишаєш здачу як чайові.
Can I try this on?
Чи можу я це приміряти?
🗣 "Excuse me, can I try this dress on?" — "Of course! The fitting rooms are over there."
It fits perfectly!
Ідеально сидить!
🗣 "Wow, it fits perfectly! I'll take it." — "Great choice!"
It's too tight / loose.
Занадто тісно / вільно.
🗣 "These jeans are too tight. Do you have a larger size?"
Do you have a medium?
Чи є у вас розмір M (середній)?
🗣 "I usually wear a medium. Do you have this in medium?"
What's my size?
Який мій розмір? (питання до продавця)
🗣 "I'm not sure about UK sizes. Can you help me find my size?"
I'd like to return this.
Я хочу повернути це.
🗣 "I'd like to return this shirt — it has a stain on it." — "Do you have your receipt?"
It's broken / damaged.
Це зламане / пошкоджене.
🗣 "I bought this yesterday and it's already broken. Can I get a replacement?"
You've given me the wrong change.
Ви дали мені неправильну здачу.
🗣 "Excuse me, I think you've given me the wrong change. I gave you £20."
Can I speak to the manager?
Чи можу я поговорити з менеджером?
🗣 Use when you have a serious problem. / Для серйозних проблем.
This wasn't what I ordered.
Це не те, що я замовив/ла.
🗣 "Sorry, this wasn't what I ordered. I asked for the small, not the large."
I love it!
Мені це дуже подобається!
🗣 "I love it — it's exactly what I was looking for!"
What a bargain!
Яка вигідна покупка!
🗣 "£5 for this quality? What a bargain!"
It's great value for money.
Воно того варте (хороша ціна за якість).
🗣 "I know it's pricey, but it's great value for money."
It suits you!
Тобі / Вам дуже личить!
🗣 "Oh, that colour really suits you! You should get it."

💡 Smart Tips — Корисні Поради

🗣️ Be Polite!

Always say "please", "thank you", and "excuse me". British and American people really appreciate politeness.

Завжди говори "please", "thank you" і "excuse me". Це дуже важливо!

"Excuse me, can you help me?" "Thank you so much!" "Could I have..., please?"

🔤 "Could" is magic!

Use "Could I...?" instead of "Can I...?" for a more polite request. It sounds friendlier!

«Could I...?» звучить ввічливіше, ніж «Can I...?»

✅ "Could I try this on?" ✅ "Could you help me find...?" ✅ "Could I have a bag?"

🏷️ Read the labels!

Key label words: reduced (знижена ціна), special offer (спецпропозиція), BOGOF = Buy One Get One Free!

BOGOF = купи один — другий безкоштовно Reduced = ціна знижена Multi-buy = купи кілька — дешевше

📱 Online Shopping

Important online words: add to cart, checkout, wishlist, tracking number, estimated delivery.

Слова для онлайн-шопінгу

Add to cart = додати в кошик Wishlist = список бажань Tracking number = номер відстеження

🤝 Bargaining

In markets you can often negotiate! Try: "Is that your best price?" or "Would you take £...?"

На ринках можна торгуватися!

"Is that your best price?" "Would you take £10?" "Can you do any better?"

↩️ Know your rights!

In the UK and US, you usually have the right to return items within 28–30 days with a receipt. Say: "I'd like to return this, please."

Ти маєш право повернути товар протягом 28-30 днів!
