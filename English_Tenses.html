⏪ Past Tenses
📖
Past Simple
Something finished

Used for actions that started and finished in the past. It's done. Over. Finished!

Past Continuous
In progress in the past

An action was in progress at a specific moment in the past — like hitting the "pause" button on a film.

FUTURE

⏺ Present Tenses

Present Simple
Habits & facts

Used for habits, routines, and facts that are always true. Things you do regularly!

PAST
← NOW →
FUTURE

🍕 Hero sentence

⏩ Future Tenses
🚀
Future Simple
Decision or prediction

Used for quick decisions, promises, or predictions. You decide as you speak!

PAST
NOW
FUTURE →
📐 Formula
Subject + will + base verb
🍕 Hero sentence
I will eat pizza tonight.
💬 Another example
It will rain tomorrow, I think.
🔑 Signal words
tomorrow next year soon I think
🛸
Future Continuous
In progress in the future

An action will be in progress at a specific moment in the future. It's the "pause" button, but tomorrow!

PAST
NOW
~~FUTURE~~
📐 Formula
Subject + will be + verb-ing
🍕 Hero sentence

💬 Another example
This time next week, she will be flying to Paris.
🔑 Signal words
at this time tomorrow this time next week while
